Team Tracker — Privacy Policy

Football squad rotation & match tracker

App name: Team Tracker
Contact: iain.dinwoodie@gmail.com
Last updated: 3 June 2026

1. What this app does

Team Tracker is a football squad rotation and match history tool for coaches. It stores player names, squad configurations, game settings, and match results.

2. Data we collect

Local-only mode (default)

All data is stored on your device only using the app's local storage. No data is transmitted to any server. This includes:

  • Player names
  • Squad configuration and formation
  • Match history and results
  • Player statistics (appearances, goals, assists, player of the match)

Cloud backup (opt-in)

If you choose to enable cloud backup by signing in with a Google account or email/password, we store a copy of the above data in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud). This allows you to restore your data on a new device.

We do not collect device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, or advertising IDs.

3. Children's data

This app is designed for coaches and parents managing youth football teams. Player data entered into the app typically relates to minors (under 18).

  • All data is entered voluntarily by the coach or parent using the app.
  • In local-only mode, no data leaves the device.
  • In cloud-backup mode, player names are stored in Firebase Firestore under your account. We store the minimum necessary (first name or preferred name as entered by you).
  • We recommend using abbreviated names or initials if you prefer additional privacy for players.

Under GDPR and UK GDPR, the coach using this app acts as a data controller for the player information they enter. The app provides tools to export and delete all data.

4. How we use your data

Data is used only to operate the app — to display squad information, generate rotation schedules, and show match history. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud: if cloud backup is enabled, data is stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud Platform). Google's privacy policy applies to this storage.
  • Legal requirement: we may disclose data if required by law.

6. Data retention

  • Local data: stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data via your device's settings.
  • Cloud data: retained until you request deletion (see section 7).

7. Your rights and data deletion

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data.

To delete local data: Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Team Tracker → Storage → Clear data.

To delete cloud data: Sign in to the app, then contact iain.dinwoodie@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will delete your Firestore account data within 30 days. You may also delete your Firebase Authentication account via this same request.

Under GDPR you also have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your data (available via the Export function in the app)
  • Correct inaccurate data (editable within the app)
  • Object to processing (cloud backup can be disabled at any time by signing out)
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO (UK): ico.org.uk

8. Security

  • Local data is protected by your device's security mechanisms.
  • Cloud data is stored in Firebase Firestore with Firebase Authentication. Access is restricted to your account only (enforced by Firestore security rules).
  • We use HTTPS for all communications with Firebase.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For any questions or data requests, contact: iain.dinwoodie@gmail.com
